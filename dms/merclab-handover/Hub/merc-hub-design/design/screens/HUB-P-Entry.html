Symptom

Engine warning light on. Rough idle and shaking for the first 1–2 minutes after a cold start; smooth once warm. No fault on a warm restart.

3

Cause

Known software behaviour in the ME control unit (demo): after a cold start the low-pressure fuel pump is driven too weakly, fuel pressure collapses and cylinder 2 misfires first.

4

Remedy

2 options
ASoftware update of the ME control unitFirst choice · fixed 3 of 3 cars
  1. Connect a battery charger; keep at least 13.5 V throughout (about 25 min).
  2. Run the online control unit update for ME with the diagnosis system.
  3. Clear the fault memory and run the idle adaptation.
  4. Cold-start test the next morning: fuel pressure actual value at least 4.5 bar, misfire counter 0.
No parts needed.
BReplace the low-pressure fuel pumpOnly if pressure stays low after A
  1. Check fuel pressure again with the engine cold. Below 3.0 bar after the update: replace the pump.

Details

Charger on for the whole update; the update takes about 25 min. Tell the customer the first cold start after the update can take one extra second.

  • W205 C200, 85,000 km (this case)
  • W213 E200, 62,000 km and 110,000 km
