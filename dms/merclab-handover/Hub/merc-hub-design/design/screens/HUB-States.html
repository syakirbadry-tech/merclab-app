W205 M274 shaking at cold start, engine light. What first?
Assistant · from the Merc HUB

Check the fuel pressure actual value, low side, cold at idle. On our cars it read 0.6 bar against 4.5–6.5 bar, and an ME software update fixed it on 3 of 3 cars1. If you also see oil on the camshaft solenoid connector with P0016, see the forum tip2.
